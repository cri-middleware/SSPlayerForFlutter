## [7.0.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- **First release.** Live reload for a running app: save a `.sspj` and see it update. The
  `sswatch` runner watches `SpriteStudio_Source/`, reconverts what went stale and pushes the fresh
  pack into the running app over the Dart VM Service.
